Instruction:
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Defend the chocolate tower from horde of cockroach like enemies 'ChocoRoach'.
Kill and survive three waves of enemies.


Controls:
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WASD Keys			Move Player
Spacebar			Dash
Mouse				Aiming 
Left Mouse Button		Hold to shoot
Right Mouse Button		Cancel placeable in Preview Mode
V				Place allies (Supporting NPCs)
B				Place barricade
1, 2, 3				Activate buff
ESC				Pause


Cheats:
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Press F1 - F10 to apply buff
F1	Attack Up
F2	Attack Speed Up
F3	Health Up
F4	Ally Level Up
F5	Barrier Level Up
F6	Health Recover
F7	Smart Gun
F8	Freezing Shots
F9	Fast Shots
F10	Reflect Damage


Debug:
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F11		Show enemy grid
F12		Show boss grid
Insert		Show obstacles
Delete		Show hitboxes
=		Next wave (only enable after wave delay)


Screen resolution:
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Fixed at 1600x900
